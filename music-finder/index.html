What this is

MusicTip helps you find the right program to make, record, edit, and mix music — without needing to know any jargon. Search by what you want to do, filter by price or operating system, and compare your options side by side.

How to use it

Start on the home page, type what you're after (or click a popular search), then narrow things down with the filters on the left. Tick "Compare" on a few programs to see them side by side. Every "Visit Website" button takes you to the software maker's official site.

A note on honesty

Prices and links change over time, so always double-check on the official site before buying. The star ratings shown here are indicative, to help you compare at a glance — they're not pulled from a live review database.
